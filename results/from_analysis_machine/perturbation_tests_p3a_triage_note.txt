P3a dataset selection (SETTINGS.md, P3: if more than 40 datasets qualify, the 40 with the most samples are kept, ties broken
by GSE number, and the rest are listed).

1. Search: the P1b search pattern for each of the 223 network miRNAs (P3a/P3a_search_log.tsv): 1,253 GEO series
   (P3a/P3a_hits.tsv). The first-pass screen counted, per series, the human samples naming a miRNA next to a
   perturbation word and the control samples; 492 series had >= 2 of each.
2. Candidates already analysed: the human P1b and P2 contrasts.
3. Reading order. For every screened series the largest possible contrast (largest miRNA-perturbation group of a cell
   type plus that cell type's control groups) was estimated from the sample annotations. The 52 series with an estimate
   of >= 10 samples, and the 70 series with the most screen-counted samples, were read, and their contrasts were set by
   the P1-P3 dataset rules (P3a/p3a_triage.tsv).
   - The contrasts range from 23 samples (GSE115646, HepG2: 17 mock controls) down to 10.
   - The 40th contrast has 10 samples. At 10 samples, 9 contrasts tie: GSE46039, GSE53143 (P1b), GSE121892 (x2),
     GSE133527, GSE196161, GSE206584, GSE243063 and GSE271495. The first 7 by GSE number are kept.
   - Series whose estimate is below 10 samples cannot enter the 40. They were not read further and are listed in
     DATASETS.tsv as "not read".
4. The perturbed mature miRNA maps to its network node by removing the arm suffix. Mimics of miRNAs that are not network
   nodes are not used, and neither are arms whose family member is not named.
5. Composition of the 40: 25 are HepG2 contrasts of one series (GSE115646) that share its 17 mock controls, and these
   contrasts are not independent. The fixed rule does not weight by series. Per-dataset values are in
   P3/p3a_per_dataset.tsv.
